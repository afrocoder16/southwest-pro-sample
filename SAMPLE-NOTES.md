## 3. The fall promo (sample offers)

You asked for a fall-themed promo under the hero based on what painting companies run in autumn. I researched it on 7 Nov 2026.
Almost every example found was a franchise page (mostly CertaPro locations), and nearly all had expired, so treat this as a
pattern, not as market data.

What companies commonly run:

- **Winter interior specials** for projects booked in the slow months, for example 10-15% off or free paint on interiors
  scheduled December to February ([Alpharetta GA](https://certapro.com/alpharetta/?p=2862), [Main Line PA](https://certapro.com/bryn-mawr/special-offers), [Portland](https://certapro.com/portland/?p=1769)).
- **"Pick two, save a flat amount"** bundles, for example Pick 2, Get $200 Off ([Lehigh Valley](https://certapro.com/greater-lehigh-valley/community/fall-into-savings/)).
- **Dollar-threshold offers**, for example $200 off $2,000 interior estimates ([Minneapolis South](https://certapro.com/minneapolis-south/?p=9249)).
- **One room free** on interior work ([Owings Mills MD](https://certapro.com/owings-mills/?p=2104)).
- **Early-booking savings on next spring's exterior work**, for example 10% off if the estimate is booked by mid-May ([Olympia WA](https://certapro.com/olympia/?p=9732)).
- **Free color consultation** with an interior estimate (several franchises).
- **"Mention the offer at your estimate"** as the redemption method, and fall framed as the time to fix exteriors before winter
  and to book while crews have capacity.

What the sample shows (three offer cards styled as autumn paint chips, plus a free color consultation perk):

| Offer | Amount | Ends |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Winter interior refresh | 10% off | projects Dec to Feb |
| Pick two, save | $200 off | Feb 28 |
| Book spring early | 5% off | estimate by Mar 31 |

**All amounts, dates and the color-consultation perk are placeholders.** The section is labelled "Sample offers" on the page, and
claiming an offer carries its name into the estimate form marked "(sample offer)". The company must decide the real offers and
terms. Note that on 7 Nov outdoor painting season in southwest Minnesota is essentially over, which is why the offers lean to
interiors and spring booking.

## 6. How the before/after viewer works (and why two modes)

Most pairs are not shot from the same spot. A drag slider only looks right when both photos line up, so:

- **Slider ("roller reveal")**: house, red barn, front door, kitchen. Drag the orange roller left to paint in the "after".
  A real range input sits over the photo, so keyboard and screen readers work.
- **Flip with a wipe**: white barn, wood siding, red garage. A Before/After switch, with a note that the angles differ.

Each job's thumbnail is a paint-chip card. Reviews sit beside the viewer on the home page and on `/homes`.

## 7. Tests run, and what was not verified

Run with `npm run qa` against the production build (see the final message for the last result): every page at 1440 / 820 / 390 px
(overflow, console errors, broken images, axe WCAG A/AA), then every interaction, plus the rules in section 1.

Lighthouse (mobile emulation, home page, lab numbers on this PC): Accessibility 100, Best Practices 100, Performance 89
(SEO 66 only because the page is `noindex` on purpose). Performance work this round: render after the key fonts load (one
layout instead of several), `content-visibility` on below-the-fold sections, lazy-loaded viewer images.

Not verified:

- Browsers other than Chrome (Safari, Firefox, Samsung Internet) and real phones (phone testing is Chrome device emulation).
- Screen readers (axe passes, but a human pass has not been done), and the roller slider on a real touchscreen.
- The falling-leaf animation on low-power phones (it is transform-only and switches off for reduced-motion users).
- Anything about real delivery: the forms send nothing, so there is no email, CRM or spam protection to test.
